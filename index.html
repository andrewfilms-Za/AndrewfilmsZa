About Andrew Films

Andrew Films is a Cape Town-based photography and film production company founded by Andrew Bryton. We specialize in cinematic storytelling through professional photography, videography, music videos, event coverage, portrait sessions, commercial content, documentaries, and short film production.


Our mission is to transform real moments into powerful visual stories that inspire, entertain, and preserve memories for generations. Every project is approached with creativity, professionalism, and attention to detail.


At Andrew Films, we believe every story deserves to be told beautifully.
